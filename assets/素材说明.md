# 手绘植物素材

使用内置 image_gen 工具生成，未使用 CLI / API key。文件：plants.png。PNG 尺寸 1536 × 1024，包含透明 Alpha 通道。

六种植物为嫩芽、蕨叶、宽叶植物、黄色雏菊、白色雏菊、细长叶枝。运行时在 Canvas 中从原图取源矩形，不修改原素材。雨滴、白色拖尾、涟漪和溅射用实时 Canvas 绘制，以支持运动、碰撞和联动。

最终生成提示词：

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: transparent PNG sprite sheet for a live webcam rain-and-growing-plants effect. Create one wide 1536x1024 transparent image with EXACTLY SIX distinct isolated small cartoon plants arranged in a single horizontal row, evenly spaced in six equal-width cells, all bottom-aligned at the same baseline, generous transparent gaps between each plant, nothing touching the image edges. Each plant fully fits its own one-sixth-width column. Plants left to right: 1 small mint-green two-leaf sprout, 2 curving sage fern, 3 broad teal-green leaf plant, 4 cheerful butter-yellow daisy with green leaves, 5 small white daisy with blue-green leaves, 6 tall wavy mint-green leafy sprig. Style: delightful hand-painted children's picture book, wax crayon edges, colored pencil hatch texture, matte gouache fills, imperfect hand-drawn organic outlines, muted mint/jade greens and soft butter yellow. Each plant grows directly from its thin stem, no pots, no soil, no ground, no shadows, no text, no labels, no border. The plants occupy lower 80% of canvas. Background must be genuinely transparent with alpha; do not paint a checkerboard. This is game production art, individually composited sprites; clearly separate all six subjects.
